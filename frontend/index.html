Best Practice Storage & Preservation Guides

🥛

Dairy & Milk

Store milk and cheeses on interior refrigerator shelves rather than the door shelves to avoid temperature fluctuations each time the door opens.

🥕

Vegetables & Herbs

Store leafy greens with a dry paper towel to absorb excess moisture. Blanch and freeze surplus vegetables in portion sizes before they turn soft.

🍲

Cooked Meals & Leftovers

Use clear, airtight containers labeled with the cooking date. Keep leftovers near the front of the fridge and designate one dinner a week as "Use-It-Up Night".

🍞

Bread & Bakery

Keep bread at room temperature in a bread box or freeze slices directly. Never refrigerate bread as it accelerates starch crystallization and makes it go stale.
